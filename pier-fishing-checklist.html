Pier Fishing Gear Checklist

Updated October 18, 2026 · Simple Tides guides

Pier fishing is the most accessible saltwater fishing there is — no boat, no kayak, just walk out and drop a line. But piers have their own gear logic: long drops, rail space, and crowds. Here's everything to bring.

Rods and reels for the pier

Bring two outfits: a 7–8 ft medium rod for working lures and live bait near the pilings, and a 9–10 ft heavier rod for casting distance and bigger baits off the end. A 4000-size reel and a 6000-size reel cover both.

Pier rod setup

A 9 ft medium-heavy spinning combo is the single most versatile pier outfit — long enough for distance, strong enough for kings and cobia.

Rigs that work from a pier

  • Gotcha plugs / spoons: for Spanish mackerel and bluefish — cast and retrieve fast.
  • Sabiki rigs: catch your own bait (greenbacks, pinfish) right off the pier.
  • Carolina rig with live bait: the default bottom rig for reds, trout and flounder.
  • Kingfish rig: wire leader and treble hooks for live-baiting toothy fish off the end.

The pier kit

  • Pier net or gaff (check pier rules — many require nets, not gaffs)
  • Bucket with rope for hauling water and landing fish
  • Rod holders that clamp to the rail
  • Pliers, dehooker, knife
  • Cooler for bait and catch
  • Chair — you'll be glad you brought one
  • Sunscreen, hat, polarized sunglasses
  • Measuring tape (slot limits are enforced)

Pier etiquette

  • Don't cast over other people's lines — walk to open rail space.
  • Yield the end of the pier ('kingfish corner') to live-baiters during the run.
  • Help net your neighbor's fish; they'll return the favor.
  • Clean up cut bait and line — monofilament kills birds.
  • Buy bait at the pier shop when you can; it keeps piers open.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best bait for pier fishing?

Live shrimp is the universal pier bait — everything eats it. Catch greenbacks or pinfish on sabiki rigs for bigger predators, and keep cut mullet for bottom rigs.

Do I need a fishing license to fish from a pier?

Most public ocean piers in states like Florida cover you under the pier's blanket license, but rules vary by state and pier. Always check the pier's posted rules and your state's regulations.

What is the best time to fish from a pier?

The two hours on either side of a tide change, especially dawn and dusk. Moving water concentrates bait and predators around the pilings.

Pier net or gaff — which one?

A pier net for most fish; it's required on many piers and safer for catch-and-release. A gaff is only for fish you intend to keep, where pier rules allow it.
