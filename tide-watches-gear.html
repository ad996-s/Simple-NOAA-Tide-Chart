Tide Watches & Tide Clocks: Top Picks

A tide watch puts the ocean’s schedule on your wrist: no phone, no signal, no guessing. Whether you’re wading a flat, timing a surf session, or running an inlet, the right tide timepiece keeps you on the water’s clock.

Why a dedicated tide watch

Phone apps are fine at the dock, but salt water, dead batteries, and glare make them a liability in the field. A tide watch reads the tide at a glance — set it once for your home coast and it tracks the cycle for years. Wall tide clocks do the same job for the beach house, the shop, or the boat’s cabin.
